Historic Cemetery

The cemetery of Lubavitch contains historic matzeivos and remains a major focus of preservation, research, cleaning, uncovering, documentation, and restoration.

Historical significance: Historic gravestones and burial grounds are central to the village's memory.

Restoration status: Preserved and documented
